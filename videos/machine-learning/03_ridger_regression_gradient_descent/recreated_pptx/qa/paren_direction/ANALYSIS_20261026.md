## Law statement (grounded by this lane)

Hand-drawn parentheses must be single-stroke curves whose BELLY faces the
enclosed content: left paren "(" has its belly leftmost (tip right of body),
right paren ")" has its belly rightmost (tip left of body). Every generator
must encode tip/body per side (never mirror one stroke across sides), and
every generated asset passes `check_paren_direction.py` before embedding.

`~/Projects/ahmed-skills/skills/slide-recreation/references/patterns/delimiters-direction-check/`.
