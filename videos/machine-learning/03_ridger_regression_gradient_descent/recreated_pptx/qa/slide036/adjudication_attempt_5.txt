Two genuine defects, both confirmed against the original:

1. **The double arrow OVERLAPPED group 2's "(W".** The generator drew
   `T(349, MAIN_BASE, "_i) (W")` — group 2's "(W" at x349-385 — and the arrow at
   `T(396, ...)`, whose ink starts at x385 and runs to x411. The arrow therefore
   struck through group 2's paren+W. That is exactly the duplicated/mis-grouped
   look the judge reported, and the ASCII read shows the arrow's `##############`
   bars sitting directly on the W column.

2. **Group 2's raised `^T` was never drawn.** The generator draws a superscript T
   only for group 1 (`T(324, MAIN_BASE-8, "T", ...)`); group 2's `X` runs were
   drawn with no `^T` at all, so group 2 rendered as plain `(W X_i)`.
   Superscript-band census (y364..374) confirms: REND had ONE cluster (group 1's
   T at 323..330); the original has its group-2 T at ~468..473.

3. **First-term closing paren.** Separately, the frame has NO closing paren on
   the first term (ink-run census shows nothing between its subscript i at
