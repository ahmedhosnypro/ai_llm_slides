**Primary‑source statements (Vaswani et al., “Attention Is All You Need”, arXiv:1706.03762)**  

- **Why RNNs cannot be parallelized over time steps**  
  > “Aligning the positions to steps in computation time, they generate a sequence of hidden states 𝒉ₜ, as a function of the previous hidden state 𝒉ₜ₋₁ and the input for position t. **This inherently sequential nature precludes parallelization within training examples**, which becomes critical at longer sequence lengths…”  
  > (Section 3.2, “Self‑Attention”; the quoted sentence appears in the discussion of recurrent layers.)

- **Why self‑attention/Transformers can be parallelized**  
  > “As noted in **Table 1**, a self‑attention layer **connects all positions with a constant number of sequentially executed operations** …”  
  > (Section 3.2, directly preceding Table 1.)

- **Complexity comparison (exact Table 1 entries)**  

| Layer type      | Complexity per layer | Sequential operations | Maximum path length |
|-----------------|----------------------|-----------------------|---------------------|
| **Self‑Attention** | 𝑂(𝑛²·𝑑)          | 𝑂(1)                  | 𝑂(1)                |
| **Recurrent**      | 𝑂(𝑛·𝑑²)          | 𝑂(𝑛)                  | 𝑂(𝑛)                |
| *(Convolutional – for reference)* | 𝑂(𝑘·𝑛·𝑑²) | 𝑂(1) | 𝑂(𝑛/𝑘) |

(Here 𝑛 = sequence length, 𝑑 = representation dimensionality, 𝑘 = kernel width.)
